# User Personas

## P1: Primary User — Job Seeker / Career Switcher

**Goal:** Understand network to find opportunities, identify relevant contacts, track outreach.

**Data:** LinkedIn export with connections, messages, job applications, saved jobs.

**Needs:**
- Who in my network works at target companies?
- Which connections are dormant and worth reactivating?
- What's my outreach response rate?
- Which people could introduce me to someone at Company X?

**Pain:** Manual LinkedIn browsing. No unified view. Can't ask natural questions.

---

## P2: Sales / Business Development Professional

**Goal:** Track relationships, identify warm introductions, measure outreach effectiveness.

**Data:** LinkedIn export plus CRM data (future).

**Needs:**
- Relationship strength across contacts
- Communication patterns and response rates
- Company engagement history
- Who can introduce me to Decision Maker Y?

**Pain:** CRM data stale. LinkedIn limited. No intelligence layer connecting people to opportunities.

---

## P3: Recruiter / Talent Acquisition

**Goal:** Map candidate networks, track pipeline, identify passive candidates.

**Data:** LinkedIn export plus job postings.

**Needs:**
- Network mapping by skill, company, role
- Communication history with candidates
- Relationship progression tracking
- Who knows whom for warm outreach

**Pain:** Scattered across spreadsheets and LinkedIn messages.

---

## P4: Researcher / Analyst

**Goal:** Analyze professional network patterns, industry connections, talent flow.

**Data:** LinkedIn export (multiple users aggregated with consent).

**Needs:**
- Network analytics across dimensions
- Industry/company/role distribution
- Communication pattern analysis
- Relationship graph visualization

**Pain:** No tool exists for personal-network analysis at scale.

---

## Anti-Persona: Data Harvester

**Not our user.** System rejects bulk scraping, unauthorized data collection, cross-tenant access. Every user owns only their own data.
